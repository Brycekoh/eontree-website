## HOW IT WORKS (4 steps)

**Section eyebrow:** What working with us looks like
**Section heading:** From idea to fully running, in four steps.

1. **We sit down with you**
   Tell us what you want the space to do. We walk the property, look at
   your existing wiring and network, and figure out what's realistic.

2. **We design the system**
   You get a clear plan — what goes where, which brands, what it does,
   and what it costs. No surprises on install day.

3. **We install and integrate**
   Our team fits everything and, the important part, makes it all talk
   to each other through one app. Tested before we leave.

4. **We show you how, and stick around**
   We walk you through it until it's second nature, and we're a call
   away if anything needs tuning or expanding later.
